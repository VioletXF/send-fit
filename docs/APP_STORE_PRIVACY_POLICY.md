# SendFit Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 29, 2026**

SendFit compresses videos locally on an iPhone or iPad. This policy describes information handling in the app and does not replace the privacy policies of Apple, Google, or other services used on a device.

## Video and files

Videos selected from Photos, Files, or a compatible system open/share flow are processed on the device. SendFit does not upload videos, operate a video-processing server, sell video content, or use video content for advertising.

To process reliably, SendFit may make a temporary copy in its own app storage. It never overwrites or deletes the original source video. Temporary working files are deleted when they are no longer needed; saved or exported videos are controlled by the user in the destination they choose.

## Advertising and consent

The free version includes Google Mobile Ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and process device, diagnostic, and advertising-related information under Google's own policies to deliver and measure ads. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests and stores applicable advertising-consent choices and presents privacy options when required.

SendFit does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization and does not access the IDFA. Advertising can require network access; video compression remains available when advertising, consent, or network access is unavailable.

## Permissions

SendFit requests access only when needed to import a video from Photos or to use a file selected through the system picker. The app uses the selected video to provide compression and export features.

## Data retention and sharing

SendFit does not maintain a user account or a first-party backend for videos or personal profiles. The app does not share video content with SendFit. Advertising-related processing is provided by Google as described above.

## Children

SendFit is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact that service directly.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change when the app's features or providers change. The current version is published in this repository. For support or privacy questions, open an issue at [VioletXF/send-fit](https://github.com/VioletXF/send-fit/issues).
