# SendFit App Review Notes

## App purpose

SendFit compresses one locally selected video to a user-chosen maximum file size. It has no user account, no server-side video processing, and no in-app purchase flow.

## Reviewer path

1. Launch SendFit and select a short video from Photos or Files.
2. Choose a size target such as 10 MB.
3. Tap Compress and wait for the progress screen.
4. On completion, use Save, Export to Files, or Share to verify the result.

## Advertising and consent

Google UMP may present a consent form depending on the reviewer’s region. A banner may appear during compression. A full-screen interstitial is eligible only at a natural post-result transition, after frequency limits; it may not appear in a short review session. Ads and consent are optional side effects and never block importing, compression, saving, or sharing.

## Privacy

Videos are processed entirely on-device. SendFit does not upload videos or operate a video-processing backend. See `APP_STORE_PRIVACY_POLICY.md` for the full policy.
