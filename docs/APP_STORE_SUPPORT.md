# SendFit Support

SendFit compresses one video on-device to a maximum file size you choose.

## Getting started

1. Select a video from Photos or Files, or open a compatible video in SendFit from another app.
2. Choose a maximum output size.
3. Tap Compress.
4. Save the result, export it to Files, or share it with another app.

## Common questions

**Does SendFit upload my video?** No. Compression runs on your iPhone or iPad.

**Why is the output smaller or lower quality?** SendFit adjusts video bitrate and, when necessary, resolution to stay within the maximum size you selected.

**Why don't I see an ad?** Ads depend on consent, network availability, inventory, and conservative frequency limits. They never block compression.

**A file will not import.** Confirm it is a supported video file and that the source remains available until importing finishes.

## Contact

For a reproducible issue, include the iOS version, SendFit version, input video duration/resolution, selected size target, and a description of the result. Do not attach private videos unless you choose to do so. Open a GitHub issue at [VioletXF/send-fit Issues](https://github.com/VioletXF/send-fit/issues).
